They were promised seven days.

Andrew and Nina signed up for a simple medical trial: revert to twenty, just for a week, then return to their sixty-year-old selves. What could go wrong?

The trial. The lab accident. The treatments got crossed — and each woke up in the body the other was meant to have.

Now Andrew is looking out through twenty-year-old female eyes, and Nina is trapped in a young man’s frame. Seven days to fix it… if the trial works the way it was supposed to.

But some things, once done, can’t be undone.

A novel · about 330 pages · Heartfelt Romance edition

If you liked The Time Traveler’s Wife, this is in that territory: a married couple, a love that has to survive an impossible condition — and the romance, not the mechanism, doing the work.

About the author

Jona Zeidler writes romances about love that lasts. His first novel, Reversible, is an homage to forty-one years of marriage — to the idea that the best love stories aren’t about finding someone new, but about finding each other again. When he’s not writing, he’s tending his hives.
